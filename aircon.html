Why one team matters

Two jobs, one licensed team.

An air conditioner is two jobs: the refrigeration and the electrical. Many installs need an aircon tech and a separate electrician.

Our ARCtick and electrical licences mean we do both, so the unit, the circuit and the switchboard are all handled together.
