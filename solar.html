How a system works

Sun by day. Battery by night.

Panels make power during the day. Your home uses it first, a battery stores what's left for the evening, and anything beyond that goes back to the grid.

The right size depends on when you use power, not just how much. That's why we look at your bills before we design anything.

  • Connection paperwork with the network handled for you

Home battery discount

The Cheaper Home Batteries Program

Batteries installed from 1 July 2025 may be eligible for the federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program, which cuts the upfront price through the installer. To qualify, the battery must be new, paired with solar, between 5 and 100 kWh, on the Clean Energy Council's approved list, and installed by an accredited installer. One discount per home. The amount is set by the program and has been stepping down since May 2026, so we confirm what applies to you when we quote.
