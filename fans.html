Ceiling fans

Quiet, secure, no wobble.

Indoor and outdoor fans, with or without lights, on wall controls or remotes. We check the ceiling can take the fan and mount it securely so it runs quietly and doesn't wobble.

Exhaust fans

Clear the steam, stop the mould.

A bathroom or laundry fan that's too weak, noisy or venting into the roof space leaves moisture behind, and moisture leads to mould. We install fans sized for the room and duct them outside where the space allows.
